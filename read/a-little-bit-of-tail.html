Prologue

Even if she could move, she knew turning around or walking away would not solve the problem, she couldn't budge. She had been his fool and believed he was the one for her, while her girlfriends criticized and ridiculed the man she loved. It would be one of those because she damn sure was going to kill this fool. Maybe the judge would be lenient considering she would be a first time offender, she thought.

Mina blinked twice to snap out of her daze. She gently back peddled out of the room, keeping as quiet as she could so her presence remained unknown. She silently ran to the hall closet, eased it opened and reached up top. She grabbed the shoebox and pulled out the purple Crown Royal bag that housed her .380 pistol.

"Whose is it, baby girl?" Kevin's dominating voice asked, filled with passion.

That was the last Mina's heart could bear. Mina couldn't believe this shit. She tried to shake the fainting feeling that was beginning to take over her body, but she just couldn't rid the urge to pass out. This mofo brought an underage girl into my house? With the .380 now aimed at Kevin's dick, Mina noticed his knees were beginning to buckle. For the first time in a long time, he was speechless. "Baby, please believe me. I am so sorry. I love you. I never meant to hurt you. It's just you've been working so late. I promise I didn't know she was only seventeen," Kevin pleaded, killing any chance he had to relax.

Mina blinked three times to allow the last part of what had just come out of Kevin's mouth to play back. He didn't know she was only seventeen? That was the straw that broke the camel's back. The last little bit of self-control she possessed was gone. It had seeped right out of her ass like diarrhea. Her vision faded to bloodshot red and she snapped.

She had worked too hard for her shit, and had come too far to just turn the other cheek. No, this negro has to learn, she thought. And to experience this ass whooping will be his best teacher ever.

His knees crumpled as his upper body hit the floor headfirst. Mina looked at Kevin, who was no longer recognizable. His eyes were swollen shut, his nose had tripled in size, and his bottom lip had literally split in two.

Before passing out, Kevin managed to moan, "Oh God, please help me."

Mina screamed. "Help? Oh, you want help... Negro, I got your help!" She kicked him again. Mina rushed out of the bedroom, down the narrow hallway and busted into the guest bathroom, almost knocking the door off its hinges. She slung open the cherry cabinet doors and grabbed the rubbing alcohol.

I got your help right here, baby. Mama's going to get you all cleaned up.

She hurried back to the bedroom, and was standing over Kevin's body before she knew it. She looked down at him in disgust. "I can't believe I loved you. You've wasted so many years of my life. Thank God he's still alive.
