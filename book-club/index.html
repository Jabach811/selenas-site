Book club kit

A Little Bit of Tail,
for your book club

Eight questions to get your club talking. Print them, pour something, and argue about Mina.

  1. The book opens with Mina at her breaking point. What did you think of her in that first scene? Did you see her differently by the end?
  2. Jazzi is at the door in five minutes and doesn't ask a single question first. Who is your Jazzi? Have you been somebody's?
  3. Jazzi held her tongue about Kevin for two and a half years, because Mina had to see it for herself. Was she right to stay quiet?
  4. Jazzi tells Mina that loyalty "can't always be a reason to stay." Where is the line between being loyal and settling?
  5. This book makes you laugh in the middle of its hardest moments. Which line got you when you thought it shouldn't?
  6. Cherry writes about women who get knocked all the way down, then come back shining. Where does Mina's comeback really begin?
  7. Which character did you want to shake? Which one did you want to hug?
  8. If you could ask Cherry one thing about what happens next, what would it be?
