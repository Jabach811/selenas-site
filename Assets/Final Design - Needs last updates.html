Her Story

From a library card
to a byline.

The short version of a long love affair with stories.

Raised on words.
Built to bounce back.

The daughter of a single mother who told her she could do anything, Cherry was reading aloud by age five — and quietly filling notebooks with poems and songs long before the world knew her name.

By day she holds her own in banking and finance in Charlotte, North Carolina. But not even a Fortune 500 could keep her from the page. Her stories blend real-life situations and hard choices with a down-home sense of humor — and above it all, her goal is simple: for God to get the glory.

— Cherry

Where can I buy Cherry's books?

All three novels are available on Amazon in paperback and on Kindle. Tap "Buy on Amazon" on any book above to go straight to its page.

Are the novels a series or standalones?

A Little Bit of Tail and Heads Over Tails follow the same circle of women — read those two in order. Something for Nothing opens a brand-new story. Each one stands on its own, but for the full ride, start at the beginning.

Is a new book on the way?

Cherry's always writing. The surest way to hear it first is to join her circle above — new releases land there before they go anywhere else.

Can I book Cherry for an event or book club?

Absolutely — Cherry loves connecting with readers. Reach out at cherry@example.com with your dates and details.
