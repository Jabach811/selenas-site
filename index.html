Her Story

From a library card
to a byline.

The short version of a long love affair with stories.

Raised on words.
Built to bounce back.

Long before she became an author, Selena Cherry was a storyteller. The daughter of a single mother who taught her that she could do anything she set her mind to, she was reading aloud by the age of five and filling notebooks with poems, songs, and stories not long after.

By day, Selena has built a career in banking and finance in Charlotte, North Carolina. Away from the corporate world, however, she returns to the place that has always felt like home: the page.

Writing under Novels by Cherry, she creates romance and contemporary fiction inspired by real-life situations, relationships, difficult choices, faith, family, and the sometimes unexpected consequences of the decisions people make. Her stories aren't afraid of life's messier moments, but they're told with heart, a down-home sense of humor, and characters who just might remind you of someone you know — or even yourself.

Her novel A Little Bit of Tail was independently published in 2014 and introduced readers to her combination of humor, drama, and real-life complication. As she enters the next chapter of her writing career, she is expanding Novels by Cherry with new stories and new characters — while staying committed to the kind of storytelling that made her want to write in the first place.

At the heart of Selena's work is her faith and a simple purpose: for God to get the glory. When she isn't writing or working, she's with family, lost in a good book, travelling, or finding a reason to laugh out loud.

Come for the books.
Stay for the stories.

— Cherry

A Little Bit of Tail and Heads Over Tails follow the same circle of women — read those two in order. Something for Nothing opens a brand-new story. Each one stands on its own, but for the full ride, start at the beginning.
