A Little Christmas Magic In The Works

Happy Holidays from Novels by Cherry

Well, y'all, it's officially safe to say happy holidays! And since Christmas is one of my absolute favorite times of the year, I figured it was only right that I share a little gift with my readers.

No, it's not wrapped in pretty paper or topped with a big red bow, but I promise it's something I'm excited about.

I'm officially writing my very first Christmas romance novel.

Now, I don't want to give too much away just yet, but let's just say there's music, a little holiday magic, and a certain someone who may discover that falling in love is one thing he can't quite control. After all, even the most successful people can find themselves completely unprepared when love comes knocking.

I'm having so much fun developing this story, and although it's still a work in progress, I'm hoping to have it ready just in time for Thanksgiving 2027.

In the meantime, I've been tossing around a few possible titles, and I thought — why not let the readers in on the fun?

So here's where you come in. Based on these titles, can you guess what the story might be about? And more importantly, which title would make you want to pick up the book? I love hearing from my readers, and your thoughts just might help me make the final decision.

This is only the beginning of what I hope will be a wonderful new chapter in my writing journey. Who knows? Maybe one Christmas romance will turn into a holiday tradition we can look forward to year after year.

Until then, I'll be working behind the scenes, bringing these characters to life, and sharing a few surprises along the way. Thank you for continuing to support my stories and for being part of the Novels by Cherry family. I can't wait to share what's coming next.

Wishing you love, laughter, and a little Christmas magic.
